Descriptive sensitivity of the saved Liverpool external scores to the target material-family composition. Let w denote the weight assigned to gellan gum (GG), with 1−w assigned to polylysine (peK). Within each family, the previously declared material-group and within-group curve averages are retained. (A) R(w)=w R_GG+(1−w)R_peK for the frozen candidate and fixed Gaussian controls. (B) Candidate-minus-control CRPS contrasts; negative values favour the candidate. The dotted line marks equal family weights (w=0.5); the dashed line marks the original equal-five-group endpoint (w=3/5). Diamond markers identify analytic equality points, w=0.390181 for the reference GP and w=0.547129 for the conditional Gaussian. The alternative estimand does not replace the predeclared primary endpoint. No model, forecasts, labels or weights were fitted for this analysis. These family weights are a descriptive population-composition variable, not model parameters, material classifiers or calibration guarantees.
