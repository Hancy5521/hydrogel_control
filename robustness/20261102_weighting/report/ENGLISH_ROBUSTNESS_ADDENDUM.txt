Saved-score weighting sensitivity (descriptive addendum)

The Liverpool external endpoint originally assigned equal weight to five nominal composition groups, comprising three gellan-gum (GG) groups and two polylysine (peK) groups. A descriptive sensitivity analysis retained that endpoint and all fixed forecasts, but separately considered equal GG/peK family weights. This reweighting changes the target composition of the loss average; it is not a new test cohort, a correction to the original endpoint, or model fitting.

Under equal family weights, candidate log-response CRPS was 0.312928, compared with 0.340916 for the reference GP and0.295105 for the conditional Gaussian. The candidate therefore retained an 8.21% CRPS improvement against GP but incurred 6.04% higher CRPS than the conditional Gaussian. Its log-RMSE was 0.616247, versus 0.610641, 0.608899 and0.601002 for GP, conditional Gaussian and the six-anchor power-law point control. The point-error advantage consequently did not persist under this alternative estimand. Point controls were assigned no invented probability scores.

Let w denote the target GG-family weight. The candidate-minus-control CRPS contrast is affine in w. Equality occurs at w=0.390181 against GP and w=0.547129 against the conditional Gaussian; candidate CRPS is lower above each corresponding threshold. These thresholds describe the saved benchmark risk function, not an estimated population prevalence, an optimized model parameter, a new-material classifier or a generalization guarantee. The predeclared five-group endpoint corresponds to w=0.6 and is not replaced.

Candidate marginal 90% coverage averaged 71.56% under the original endpoint and 64.44% under equal family weights. The family-level contrast was 100% coverage for GG and 28.89% for peK; neither certifies calibration. Arithmetic deletion of a saved group retained the candidate's lower GP CRPS in all five deletions, but removing GG 0.5 or GG 0.75 reversed its comparison with the conditional Gaussian. This operation used fixed group scores and involved no leave-one-out retraining or independent confirmation.

All four external sources remain reported. Candidate CRPS did not beat the reference GP on HA/CMHA solutions, granular hydrogels or dynamic crosslinked alginate; the latter remained a severe failure. The narrow retrospective contribution and the original controlled comparisons are retained, while external advantages are explicitly conditioned on the reported material composition. Preparation-batch independence and exchangeability were not established, so no significance test, population confidence interval or calibration guarantee is asserted from two families or five composition groups.
