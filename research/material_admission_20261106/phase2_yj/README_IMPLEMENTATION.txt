Native CRPS numerical scope
Raw t/Gaussian reuse unchanged parent closed scores. Transported t/Gaussian use
standard latent variable t and integrand CDF(t)^2 or SF(t)^2 times
 s0*scale*gprime(location+scale*t).
The target t and inverse0switch are explicit integration cuts; fixed subdivision
points[-16,-8,-4,-1,0,1,4,8,16] avoid missing narrow central mass. Right tail usesSF.
Each score saves subinterval integrals/quaderr, standard truncation cut, tail
doublings and both ideal-arithmetic analytic log-tail bounds. Overall requested
native absolute/relative tolerance is1e-8/1e-8. Each outside tail receives1/8
absolute budget; perpiece quad budgets are divided by4*number_of_pieces. Initial
cut at least16 and beyond target/switch, max1e6/max16doublings, quadlimit200.
Numerical refinement is predetermined, not model search. A warning/nonconvergence
or failed error budget is a retained stop, not a dropped row or score clipping.

Student-t envelope uses SF(t)<=Cnu*t^(-nu),
Cnu=cnu*nu^((nu-1)/2). Gaussian envelope uses SF(t)<=phi(t)/t.
On right tail gprime<=1+abs(location)/2+scale*t/2; on left beyond switch gprime<=1.
These bound truncation tails analytically in ideal arithmetic. Logbounds are
retained even if their numerical display underflows. Quaderr is an estimator;
this is not directed IEEE arithmetic or a certificate of scipy CDF error. The
independent numerical gate must check the actual score implementation, including
both latent-normal and latent-t transports. No old Gaussian-mixture native score
or star-rho/moment/readout-transport certificate is silently inherited.
