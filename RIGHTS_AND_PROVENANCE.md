# Rights and provenance

This checkpoint was archived to the user-designated repository. Repository visibility alone does not grant a new software, manuscript or data reuse license. No MIT/Apache/CC license is invented for the present authors' work; the authors must approve a final license and publication declarations. NumPy/SciPy and other third-party binaries are not bundled.

The reported rheology source is Colin Zhang's dataset version 0.0.2, DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19602891>, associated with Zhang, Elvitigala and Sakai, “Generative AI-guided in silico closed-loop optimisation of deposition morphology for 3D bioprinting applications”, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1080/17452759.2026.2671497>. Inherited source records identify CC BY4.0 for the dataset; verify the actual source terms before redistributing further material. This repository does not mirror the raw dataset or original source implementation.

The inference runtime/model and the two manuscript DOCX files are original checkpoint bytes, identified by hashes. They do not contain newly measured materials. The synthetic anchor example is labelled as generated demonstration data. Result summaries are copied arithmetic evidence from already-consumed 25 P development; copying them is not another experiment.

The manuscript explicitly attributes Gaussian conditioning, inducing-feature and full-residual representations, Student-t processes and proper scoring methods to prior work. Its references remain part of the author-review draft. Of 48 reference identity checks, 40 had official metadata support and 8 were left access-unknown after rate limits/challenges; this is not a claim of reading 48 full papers or of completing 100 papers in each requested literature group.

The repository is a public checkpoint, not journal publication, peer review, authorship confirmation or acceptance certification. Original large archives and internal execution receipts remain local; the public manifests distinguish hosted files from local-only witnesses.
