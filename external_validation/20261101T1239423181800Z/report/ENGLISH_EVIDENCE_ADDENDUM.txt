External source transport assessment (additional evidence; original model unchanged)

Four separately sourced experimental datasets provided 46 native curves, 23 conservative material analysis groups and 781 continuation targets. The fixed candidate, its reference GP and an existing conditional Gaussian were evaluated without retraining. Six lowest in-support native rates were observed; all remaining admitted native rates were scored. Anchor-only PCHIP and two power-law point controls used the same information. Source inspectors had examined the responses; this was a source-inspected retrospective transport assessment, not a blinded same-formulation new-batch confirmation.

On Liverpool inks (15 runs in five composition groups), the candidate achieved group-macro log-response CRPS0.273635, versus0.327109 for the reference GP and0.293629 for the conditional Gaussian. Its group-macro log-RMSE was0.562155, versus0.597078 and0.607591. These gains did not imply calibration: average marginal90% coverage was71.56%, and the peK10% group had zero coverage. Composition-level decomposition is descriptive, not a separately held-out confirmation.

The candidate did not outperform its reference GP in CRPS on the HA/CMHA, granular-hydrogel or dynamic crosslinked-alginate sources. The latter showed severe failure (CRPS3.302394; coverage2.44%). All sources and eligible curves were retained. Chemistry, state and acquisition protocol are confounded, so these results do not establish a unique failure mechanism. They support a bounded external ink continuation result and reveal transfer limits; they do not establish general polymer transfer, independent calibration or measured experimental savings.

Scores first average target positions within a native curve, then native curves/conditions within the declared analysis group, then groups within a source. A group is not a certified independent preparation batch; concentration variants are not experimental replicates. The reported log-RMSE is the square root of group-macro MSE, not a pooled RMSE. Point controls receive no invented probabilistic scores. Independent arithmetic, CDF-integral and dense-Gaussian checks passed without changing the frozen forecasts.
